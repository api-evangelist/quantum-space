# Quantum Space

> Quantum Space is a space defense and orbital mobility company. It is not a quantum computing company. Its Ranger platform is a maneuverable spacecraft designed to operate across LEO, MEO, GEO, and cislunar orbits, serving national security, civil, and commercial customers.

Key facts about Ranger:
- Up to 4,000 kg of storable propellant
- Multi-mode (chemical and electric) propulsion
- Up to 12 km/s max delta-V
- Designed for a 15-year on-station operational life
- Supports on-orbit refueling, both giving and receiving fuel
- Modular payload architecture (5 mission-configurable ports)

Quantum Space is headquartered in Rockville, Maryland, with manufacturing in Hawthorne, California; integration in Huntsville, Alabama; and production in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Leadership includes Jim Bridenstine (CEO, former NASA Administrator), Dr. Kam Ghaffarian (Executive Chairman & Co-founder), and Kerry Wisnosky (President & Co-founder). The company's first spacecraft, a Scout satellite, launched in May 2024. The first Ranger mission, Ranger Prime, is scheduled no earlier than Q2 2027.
